California Drought Trigger Map

Drought triggers, thresholds, and response actions from California's published SB 552 county drought plans

What is SB 552?

California Senate Bill 552, signed into law September 23, 2021, directs counties to establish a drought task force and plan for their State Small Water Systems (SSWSs) and domestic wells, households not served by a city water utility. Each plan defines how the county will recognize drought and what planned actions it will take in response.

To do this, most plans identify specific "triggers", measurable conditions like a drought severity rating, a number of dry well reports, or a groundwater level, that move a county into a higher level of concern and set off a defined response, like distributing emergency water or expediting well permits.

Key Terms

Trigger — the combination of an indicator crossing a threshold that moves a county into a new drought stage and activates a specific response.
Indicator — what the county measures (e.g. groundwater level, dry well reports).
Threshold — the specific value or condition that must be met.
Stage — the drought severity level the county enters once the threshold is met.
Response action — what the county actually does once that stage is reached.
Baseline — this stage has no measurable trigger. It is the plan's default state.
